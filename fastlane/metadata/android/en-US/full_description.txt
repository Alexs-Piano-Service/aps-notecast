APS NoteCast is an Android app for practical player-piano and BLE MIDI workflows.

It lets you scan for compatible Bluetooth LE MIDI adapters, connect to the selected MIDI device, import Standard MIDI Files, organize playlists, and send playback to a player piano or other MIDI receiver.

Core features:

* Scan-first BLE MIDI connection flow
* Saved MIDI devices with quick reconnect and switching
* Local MIDI library and playlists
* Single-file and playlist playback
* Shuffle, repeat, stop-after-current, and stop-after-playlist modes
* Tempo, transpose, volume, mute, solo, and per-channel controls
* Panic controls for all notes, sound, controllers, and pedals
* BLE MIDI recording to Standard MIDI File
* MIDI export/share and library backup/restore
* Light, dark, and system appearance modes

APS NoteCast does not include analytics or advertising. Optional bug reports are sent only when the user opens the report dialog and submits one.
