APS NoteCast is an Android app for practical player-piano and BLE MIDI workflows.

It lets you scan for compatible Bluetooth LE MIDI adapters, connect to the selected MIDI device, import Standard MIDI Files, organize playlists, and send playback to a player piano or other MIDI receiver.

APS NoteCast is free and open-source. It has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising, or paid music catalog.

Demo MIDI files are from the Mutopia Project and are marked Public Domain / no rights reserved on their source pages. They are included so users can test APS NoteCast immediately.

It is not a streaming service, subscription catalog, or licensed music platform. APS NoteCast plays user-supplied MIDI files, recordings, and files the user has the right to download, import, and use.

When APS NoteCast links to external MIDI sources, those sources are labeled as external. Rights and permissions vary by source; users should treat external-source access as personal/noncommercial unless broader rights are clearly granted. Do not sell, redistribute, remaster, or use external-source files for paid/commercial playback unless allowed by the rights holder.

Core features:

* Scan-first BLE MIDI connection flow
* Saved MIDI devices with quick reconnect and switching
* Local MIDI library and playlists
* User-initiated import from local files, recordings, and external MIDI sources
* Single-file and playlist playback
* Shuffle, repeat, stop-after-current, and stop-after-playlist modes
* Tempo, transpose, volume, mute, solo, and per-channel controls
* Panic controls for all notes, sound, controllers, and pedals
* BLE MIDI recording to Standard MIDI File
* MIDI export/share and library backup/restore
* Light, dark, and system appearance modes

APS NoteCast is independent and is not affiliated with Yamaha, PianoStream, PianoDisc, QRS, Steinway, or Spirio. It does not include analytics or advertising. Optional bug reports are sent only when the user opens the report dialog and submits one.
